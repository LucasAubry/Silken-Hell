## Prompt

Create ONE production pixel-art game sprite atlas on a genuinely transparent background. EXACT regular grid of 4 columns and 3 rows, twelve equal square cells, no grid lines, no labels or text. Each object is centered with generous clear margins fully inside its cell. Preserve the chunky pixel style, top-down view, body silhouette and angular legs of the brown spider reference. All spiders face DOWN toward the viewer. Row 1 left-to-right: (1) large mature scarlet-red spider queen with irregular ivory-white spots over abdomen and head, dark burgundy shadows, amber eyes, NO EGGS attached; (2) baby red spider with a few small white spots; (3) baby ivory-white spider with red eyes; (4) baby near-black charcoal spider with dark red eyes. Row 2: (1) one intact small ivory-white spider egg, softly irregular oval, pixel shading; (2) EXACT same egg with fine initial cracks; (3) same egg with deep branching cracks; (4) empty broken white eggshell pieces scattered in a small patch, no baby. Row 3: (1) silvery-white sticky spiderweb projectile, irregular silk bundle with trailing strands; (2) round irregular wall spiderweb with eight radial threads and concentric silk; (3) tall OPEN doorway made of layered white spiderweb, dark empty transparent opening, no door panel; (4) adult ivory-white partner spider, exactly same brown reference anatomy and orange eyes but white body. Uniform pixel density, clean readable silhouettes, no outside cast shadows, no scenery, no background colors. Square overall canvas with precisely equal cell positions. This is a game asset sheet, not an illustration. Keep the boss eggs entirely separate from the boss sprite.
