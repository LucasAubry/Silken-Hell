# Nid de larves

Mode : ImageGen intégré, références magma_pool.png et magma_larva.png.
Fichier final : assets/sprites/magma_nest.png.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one simple readable pixel-art game sprite for Silken Hell: a harmless larva spawning nest, viewed from above with very slight frontal perspective, isolated on genuine transparent PNG alpha background. Reference 1 is the old hazardous-looking pool to replace, reference 2 is the existing red/green larva for art style and offspring identity only. New design: small low oval nest of muted brown woven dry fibers, three large pale ivory eggs, one cracked egg with a tiny rounded red-and-muted-green segmented grub peeking out. Simple broad silhouettes readable at 72 pixels wide, crisp pixel-art dark outlines matching the references, limited palette. Calm hatchery, safe to walk over, no liquid, no lava, no poison, no spikes, no flames, no neon glow, no threatening mouth, no floating effects, no scenery, no text. One centered complete nest with transparent margins, horizontal oval footprint.
