## Prompt

Use case: style-transfer. Create a polished transparent-background 2D game boss sprite: the hedgehog from reference 1, repainted in exactly the rich hand-painted fantasy art direction of the brown spider in reference 2. Reference 1 is the character identity and pose: preserve full body facing viewer with elevated top-down game camera, broad oval dense back of huge layered sharp cream-tipped brown quills, tiny four clawed feet, small rounded ears, cream face, black nose, determined amber eyes. Reference 2 is STYLE ONLY: smooth sculpted painted volumes, detailed warm brown materials, restrained gold edge lighting, thin dark contour, confident clean non-pixel brushwork. Match the spider's painterly 2D rendering; NOT Hollow Knight, not flat minimalist ink art, not pixel art. Keep the hedgehog threatening but charming and immediately recognizable from original. Individual large quills read crisply at small scale. Symmetrical front-facing pose, full creature entirely visible, generous clear padding on all sides. No floor, no background scene, no pedestal, no external cast shadow, no halo, no typography. Transparent alpha background. Single centered sprite, not a sheet. Sharp silhouette and crisp material details, no fog, no blur.
